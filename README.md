# Economic Outlook: Musicians & Creatives in Austin/Texas
Working paper: Eric Booth  

**Research questions:** How friendly are Texas and Austin policies, programs, and
economic opportunities for working musicians in Austin, benchmarked against
other gig workers and creatives in the Texas economy? What do we know about how much
musicians earn for gigs in Austin TX? Do any policy-relevant data capture the economic
opportunities for musicians with fidelity and if not how does this impact relevant public
policy?

Data story and rull report: https://ericabooth.github.io/EconomicOutlook_MusiciansCreatives-public/
