# Economic Outlook: Musicians & Creatives in Austin/Texas

**Started:** 2026-08-01 | **Lead:** Eric Booth (Texas 2036)

**Research question:** How friendly are Texas and Austin policies, programs, and
economic opportunities for working musicians in Austin, benchmarked against
other gig workers and creatives in the Texas economy?

**Deliverable:** ≤15-page LaTeX white paper ("State of the Austin Working
Musician, 2026" style brief), built by triangulating official statistics
(OEWS, PUMS, NES, QCEW, BEA ACPSA, SVOG, HUD/Zillow) with less-formal sources
(city grant disbursements, music census surveys, foundation reports, news
coverage of musician pay and venue closures, state policy benchmarks).

## Folder map
- `00_admin/` — research log, kickoff notes
- `01_evidence/` — raw evidence by workstream; each subfolder has
  `_sources.md` (URL, retrieval date, vintage, caveats) and `_findings.md`
  (key facts spotted during collection)
  - `01_wages_oews_qcew/` — BLS wage/employment series, music + comparison occupations
  - `02_pums_nes_microdata/` — ACS PUMS extracts, Census Nonemployer Statistics
  - `03_creative_economy_bea/` — BEA ACPSA state arts economy, NASAA appropriations
  - `04_city_programs_lmf/` — Austin Live Music Fund, CSAP, city budget/audits
  - `05_music_census_pay_surveys/` — 2014/2022 Austin Music Censuses, pay surveys, news
  - `06_state_policy_benchmark/` — TX Music Office/Incubator Rebate vs TN/GA/LA/NY
  - `07_svog_federal_relief/` — SBA Shuttered Venue Operators Grant awards
  - `08_venues_ecosystem/` — TMO directory, mixed-beverage receipts, closure timeline
  - `09_cost_of_living/` — HUD FMR, Zillow ZORI, ACS rents, BEA RPP
  - `10_apprenticeship_workforce/` — RAPIDS apprenticeship data, creative workforce programs
  - `11_streaming_royalties/` — MLC/SoundExchange/RIAA royalty-flow materials
- `02_plan/` — white paper outline, data inventory, analysis plan (written after
  evidence round; checked with Eric before analysis/writing begins)
