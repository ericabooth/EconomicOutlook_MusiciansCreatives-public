# Is "Musician" an apprenticeable occupation under DOL rules?

**Yes.** "MUSICIAN" appears on the U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship's official
list of occupations recognized as apprenticeable.

- RAPIDS occupation code: **2080CB**
- O*NET-SOC code: **27-2042.02** (Musicians, Instrumental)
- Training type: **Competency-Based** (not time-based hours)
- Guideline/Bulletin: **19-24**
- Source file cover sheet date: "Revised: March 2020" (this is the version currently linked
  from apprenticeship.gov as of retrieval date; DOL does not appear to have re-issued a newer
  cover-sheet date even though occupations are added/updated on a rolling basis via bulletins)

## Why this matters, and the caveat
Being "apprenticeable" is necessary but not sufficient. It means a sponsor (employer, union,
conservatory, venue, or joint labor-management group) *could* register a Registered
Apprenticeship program for musicians or these adjacent roles with DOL or a State Apprenticeship
Agency (Texas is a federally-administered "Office of Apprenticeship" state, not a separate SAA
state). Being on this list does **not** mean any such program currently exists in Texas, or
that one exists anywhere at meaningful scale for the "musician" title specifically — see
`04_texas_creative_workforce_check.md` and the RAPIDS totals note for what we could confirm
about actual uptake.
