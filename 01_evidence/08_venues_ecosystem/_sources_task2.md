## How to read this log

Two retrieval modes were used and they carry different evidentiary weight:

- **FETCHED** — the URL was retrieved directly with WebFetch and its content parsed. Highest confidence.
  Figures drawn from these are quoted from the page itself.
- **SEARCH** — the URL appeared in a WebSearch result and its content reached me only as a search-engine
  summary. The URL is real and the summary is derived from that page, but **I did not read the page**.
  Treat details from these as provisional. They are flagged in the CSV `notes` column where they carry a
  row.

## D. Conflicts, corrections, and coding decisions

**1. Sidewinder closure date — RESOLVED.** A search snippet framed the Sidewinder closure as September
2025. This is wrong. The fetched Chronicle article is dated **2018-05-18** and Community Impact
independently confirms closure in June 2018 reporting; the last show was **May 12, 2018**. Chronicle
article IDs corroborate the ordering (12083682 = 2011, 12098951 = Sidewinder, 12101822 = 2020). Coded
as **2018**.

**2. Threadgill's Riverside opening year — UNRESOLVED.** Search summaries give the Riverside location as
opening **1996**; Rank & Revue titles its retrospective "1981–2018". 1981 is likely the year Eddie Wilson
took over the original North Lamar restaurant, not the Riverside opening. Coded **1996** with the conflict
noted in the row.

**3. The Lost Well closure cause — MINOR CONFLICT.** Chronicle (2024-10-24) attributes it to the landlord
selling in connection with I-35 expansion. KUT (2024-12-18) attributes it to the landlord not renewing the
lease. Both agree the landlord initiated it. Coded `sale_of_property` per the Chronicle, with the
discrepancy noted.

**4. One-2-One Bar — WEAK.** Listed by the Chronicle among 2020 permanent closures, but a later search
returned listings suggesting continued South Lamar operation. The closure is coded from the Chronicle but
flagged as not independently confirmed. **Do not use this row in a headline count without re-checking.**

**5. Coconut Club — coded `at_risk`, not `closed`.** Announced for demolition spring 2022, expected to
close summer 2024, still showing operating hours in mid-2026 listings. This is a general warning about
building closure counts from closure *announcements*.

**6. Relocations coded `closed_relocated`, not `closed`.** The Lost Well, Giddy Ups, Rio Rita, Nutty Brown,
Emo's, Antone's (Riverside), and Stars Cafe all moved rather than ceased. The Lost Well and Giddy Ups have
separate `reopened` rows at their new addresses. Counting these as permanent losses — as several year-end
roundups implicitly do — overstates venue attrition.

**7. Address fields are the weakest column in the CSV.** Many are drawn from general listings rather than
from the cited article, and are marked unconfirmed in `notes`. Do not map or geocode from this file
without independent address verification.

**8. Round Rock Amp is outside Austin** (Williamson County) and flagged as such in its row. Cheatham Street
Warehouse (San Marcos) was on the research list but **no usable source was retrieved** and it has no row.
