## Summary

Documents venue turnover as succession rather than net loss. Chess Club opened at 617 Red River with
a soft launch during SXSW 2022, in the room Plush had occupied from 2000 until May 2020. It inherited
Plush's 105-person capacity.

Operators: Jason McNeely and Brian Tweedy, both formerly of Barracuda and Hotel Vegas, with Max
Vandever (Flesh Lights) and Cliff White. The people displaced by Barracuda's 2020 closure reopened
two years later, two blocks away, in a smaller room.

## Tenancy chain at 617 Red River

Blue Flamingo (1992-2000, LGBTQ punk and all-ages) -> Plush (2000-2020) -> Chess Club (2022- ).

## Quote

Tweedy described it as "a soft opening, about as soft as they come."

## Relevance

Cautions against reading a closure list as a count of lost stages. Red River addresses recycle: 617
Red River has held three named venues in three decades and has been continuously programmed except
2020-2022. The economically meaningful variables are total capacity, number of paid slots, and
operator continuity, not the count of venue names that disappear. Note also the direction of the
capacity change: Barracuda's team returned into a 105-cap room.
