## Summary

The recovery half of a displacement. The Lost Well, forced out of 2421 Webberville Rd. in August 2024
when the landlord sold, reopened at 1141 Airport Blvd. on August 15, 2025 — a twelve-month gap, timed
to the venue's 12th anniversary.

The new space is the former Tropicana. Owners Marcello Murphy and Miguel Jimenez describe it as an
upgrade on the dimensions that matter operationally: better load-in for musicians, more bar space, a
larger parking lot.

The buildout was financed largely outside the market. Murphy says roughly 90% of the work was
volunteered, using recycled materials and items donated by other bar owners and fans.

## Quote

Murphy: "90% of this thing has been volunteered."

## Relevance

Two points. First, it corrects the closure record: a venue on multiple 2024 "lost" lists is operating
again on Airport Boulevard, and the timeline should code the Webberville closure as
`closed_relocated`, not `closed`.

Second, the reopening mechanism is worth naming. A displaced venue was reconstituted through donated
labor and materials rather than through capital, insurance, or public relief. That is a real form of
resilience, but it is not a scalable or reliable one, it transfers the cost onto the scene's own
unpaid labor, and it will not be available to operators without deep community ties. The volunteer
share is a useful indicator of how thin the formal financing options for small venues are.
