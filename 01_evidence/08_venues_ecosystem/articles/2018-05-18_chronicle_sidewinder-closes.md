## Summary

The Sidewinder, a two-stage club at 715 Red River, closed May 12, 2018, less than three years after
opening in late 2015. Owner John Wickham attributed the failure to a regulatory action rather than to
rent or the market.

In the week before SXSW 2018, the Austin Fire Marshal's Office cut the occupancy of the club's back
patio from 239 to 49. Wickham said the reduction followed city staff re-interpreting what counted as
exits and entrances at the site. Because SXSW is a concentrated revenue window for Red River venues,
losing roughly 80% of outdoor capacity going into the festival removed income the club could not
recover. Erosion affecting the outdoor stage was also cited in later coverage.

The venue had opened in the former Red Eyed Fly space with partners including operators of Red 7,
which had itself closed in 2015 over rent. Community Impact separately confirmed the closure in
June 2018.

## Succession at 715 Red River

Red Eyed Fly (closed 2015) -> The Sidewinder (2015-2018) -> Birdie Num Nums (brief) -> The Cut (2023- ).

## Quote

The reduced occupancy, per the Chronicle, cost operators "significant income during the March mega
festival."

## Relevance

The best-documented Austin case of `permit_regulatory` closure. Useful as a counterweight to the
assumption that venue loss is purely a rent story: here a capacity re-interpretation, applied days
before the single largest revenue week of the year, was the proximate cause.
