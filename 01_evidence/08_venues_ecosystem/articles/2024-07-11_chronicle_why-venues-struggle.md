## Summary

The most useful single overview of the post-pandemic operating environment, and the source for
several program-funding figures.

**Red River district scale:** more than 125 local artists perform across 14 Red River venues over
three days of Hot Summer Nights. The Red River Merchants' Association covers 40+ businesses.

**Live Music Fund:** $3.5 million distributed in its first year. For 2024 the City raised individual
artist grants from the $5,000-$10,000 band to $15,000-$30,000, and added a new venue grant tier at
$30,000-$60,000. That venue tier is the meaningful change: prior rounds funded artists, not the rooms.

**Red River Special Revenue Fund:** $150,000 allocated effective the October 2024 fiscal year.

**Other:** a $45,000 Texas Commission on the Arts grant for Hot Summer Nights (July 2024).

**Venues discussed as operating:** Mohawk, Cheer Up Charlies, 13th Floor, Elysium, Valhalla, Stubb's
(2,200 cap), Chess Club (100 cap), Hole in the Wall, Beerland, Hotel Vegas, Creek and the Cave,
Empire Control Room, Swan Dive, Flamingo Cantina, Antone's.

The framing throughout is margin compression rather than a single shock: bar sales down, insurance and
permitting costs up, redevelopment approaching the club corridors.

## Quote

Mohawk founder James Moody: "Our business is growing, but it's growing from a total crater."

## Relevance

Supplies the Live Music Fund grant-tier numbers and the Hot Summer Nights artist count. The Moody
quote is the cleanest available statement that post-COVID growth figures are measured off a collapsed
base and should not be read as recovery to trend.
