## Summary

Year-end roundup. Music and nightlife entries, with stated causes:

| Business | Area | Month | Years | Reason cited |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Parlor | North Austin | April | 20+ | Landlord died; building offered at $3M, operators could not raise the ~$60,000 down payment |
| Hi Hat Public House | East Austin | July | 12 | Not stated |
| Giddy Ups | South Austin | August | 28 | Redevelopment / lease not renewed |
| The Lost Well | East Austin | October | 11 | Landlord did not renew lease |
| Outer Heaven | East Austin | announced Oct, closing early 2025 | — | Landlords demolishing building for luxury condos |

Also listed: Oat Willie's, Baby A's, The Glass Coffin, Stars Cafe, South Congress Books, Irene's.

**The Parlor is the most analytically interesting case.** It is not a rent story. The operators had
loan approval for the $3 million purchase and still lost the building over a down payment of roughly
$60,000 — about 2% of the price. A long-tenured venue with a viable business and financing in hand was
extinguished by a liquidity gap, triggered by a landlord's death. That is a capital-access failure,
not a demand failure, and it points at a different policy instrument (down-payment or acquisition
bridge financing) than rent subsidy does.

**Giddy Ups** owners characterized the redevelopment as displacement dressed up as progress.

## Relevance

Supplies four dated 2024 closures with causes. Both Giddy Ups and The Lost Well later reopened at new
addresses (June 2026 and August 2025 respectively), so this list overstates permanent loss if read
without follow-up.
