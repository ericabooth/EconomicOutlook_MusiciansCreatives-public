## Summary

The first disbursement from Austin's Iconic Venue Fund, and the clearest read on that program's
capacity relative to demand.

**The deal:** On August 15, 2023, Hole in the Wall (on the Drag, near UT, operating since about 1973)
secured a 20-year lease backed by $1.6 million in City assistance. It was the fund's first award.

**Fund mechanics:** capitalized from hotel occupancy tax revenue plus a 2018 bond package. Venues
qualify by reflecting Austin's "unique character and brand" under the founding ordinance. The fund was
established in late 2020, so the first award came roughly three years after creation.

**Demand versus supply:** the City received **45 proposals requesting over $300 million**, and
short-listed **14 venues**. One award of $1.6 million was made. Requests exceeded this award by more
than two orders of magnitude. Even allowing that requests are inflated relative to need, the ratio is
the story.

**Priority venues named in the founding resolution:** Victory Grill, Broken Spoke, Continental Club,
Hole in the Wall, Antone's, Stubb's.

**Prior fragility at this venue:** Hole in the Wall was closed from July 2002 to May 2003 and faced a
near-closure threat in 2015. It is not a venue that was never at risk.

## Quote

General manager Austin Leos: "You can see a band every single night here, and that's part of Austin's
culture."

## Relevance

Central to Deliverable 3. The $300 million in requests against a single $1.6 million award is the
sharpest available measure of unmet preservation need, and the three-year lag from fund creation to
first dollar is a program-design finding in its own right.
