## Summary

A redevelopment displacement that has been pending for years without resolving, which is itself the
point.

Coconut Club sits at Fourth and Colorado downtown. Houston-based developer Hanover Company plans a
40-story mixed-use tower with 400 residential units on the site. Fourth Street bars are to be
partially demolished with facades preserved; the Colorado Street buildings, including Coconut Club,
are to be demolished outright.

Timeline as reported: demolition announced spring 2022; venue still operating December 2023;
management expecting to close around summer 2024, with SXSW 2024 confirmed. As of mid-2026 listings
the club still shows operating hours, so the closure has slipped by at least two years past the
original expectation.

Co-owner Cole Evans and manager Joseph Aguirre are named.

## Quote

"We're going to be open for some time, and we're just going to enjoy it while we can."

## Relevance

Two analytic uses. First, it is a clean `redevelopment` case with the replacing land use quantified
(40 stories, 400 units) against a single-storey music venue, which supports a land-value argument
directly. Second, it is a caution about status coding: a venue publicly announced as closing can keep
operating for years. Any count of "venues lost" assembled from closure announcements will overstate
losses unless the announcements are re-checked against current operation. This row is coded `at_risk`,
not `closed`, for that reason.
