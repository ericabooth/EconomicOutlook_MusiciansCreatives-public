## Summary

A second succession chain, at 711 Red River.

- **Beerland** (2001-2019). Founded by Randall and Donya Stockton; sold to Richard Lynn in late 2017.
  Lynn announced a sale to an unnamed buyer on May 28, 2019; staff struck over wages unpaid since
  March. The community raised roughly $18,000 in back pay but the club did not reopen. Stubb's general
  manager Ryan Garrett took over the lease in October 2019.
- **Green Jay** (January 2020 - 2022). Opened by Garrett and Ned Stewart after renovation, weeks
  before the pandemic shutdown.
- **The 13th Floor** (September 2022 - ). Relocated from 1812 E. 12th St., where it had operated since
  March 3, 2018.

Co-owner Jake Garcia framed the move as expansion: the Red River room was large enough to add a stage
and live music, which the cramped Eastside space could not support. He also described the E. 12th St.
landlord situation deteriorating after the property passed to corporate ownership.

## Quote

Garcia on the new space: "We'll just be [the old bar] times four, basically, and with a stage."

## Relevance

Two things for the white paper. First, an Eastside operator moved *into* the Red River district in
2022, cutting against a simple downtown-hollowing narrative. Second, the stated push factor at the old
site was a shift from an individual landlord to a corporate one, which is a distinct mechanism from a
rent number and appears repeatedly in this evidence set.
