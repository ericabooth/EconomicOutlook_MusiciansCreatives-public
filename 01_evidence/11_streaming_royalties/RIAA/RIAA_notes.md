# RIAA Year-End Revenue Reports — Key Numbers

Retrieved 2026-08-01. PDFs saved: `RIAA/RIAA_2025_YearEnd_Revenue_Report.pdf`, `RIAA/RIAA_2024_YearEnd_Revenue_Report.pdf` (contrast year, 2024 data), `RIAA/RIAA_2015_YearEnd_Shipments_Memo.pdf` (contrast year, ~2015 data).

## IMPORTANT METHODOLOGY CAVEAT — basis change between reports
RIAA changed its reporting basis between the two most-recent reports:
- The **2024 Year-End Revenue Report** (published March 2025, covering CY2024) reports at **estimated retail value**: Total US revenue $17.7B; total streaming revenue $14.9B (84% of total).
- The **2025 Year-End Revenue Report** (published March 2026, covering CY2025) reports at **wholesale value**, per an explicit note on the report itself: "RIAA reports are now based on wholesale data to align with global reporting standards." Total US revenue $11.535B; total streaming revenue $9.47B (82.1% of total).
- The 2015 report (retail basis) shows total revenue $7.0B retail / $4.95B wholesale.
- **Do not directly compare 2025's $11.5B wholesale figure to 2024's $17.7B retail figure as if revenue fell** — that is a reporting-basis artifact, not a real ~35% revenue decline. For any "then vs. now" dollar comparison, use same-basis figures (retail-to-retail or wholesale-to-wholesale) or lead with the **percentage streaming share**, which is more basis-robust.

## Streaming share of US recorded music revenue, then vs. now
- **2015** (retail basis): Streaming = 34.3% of total revenue (first year streaming became the largest single format); streaming revenue crossed $2B for the first time ($2.4B combining subscription + ad-supported + SoundExchange distributions, +29% YoY). Paid subscriptions grew 52% to $1.2B; average paid subscriptions 10.8M (+40% YoY).
- **2024** (retail basis): Streaming (incl. paid subscription, ad-supported, SoundExchange, other) = 84% of total revenue, a record $14.9B, +4% YoY. Paid subscription revenue alone: $11.7B (+5%), 100M average subscriptions (first time crossing 100M), accounting for 79% of streaming revenue and ~2/3 of total revenue.
- **2025** (wholesale basis): Streaming = 82.1% of total revenue ($9.47B of $11.535B, +3.1% YoY). Paid subscriptions (premium) $5.88B (+6.8%), 106.5M accounts (+6.5%). Total US revenue hit a record $11.535B (wholesale terms), +3.1% YoY.
- **Net takeaway for the sidebar**: streaming's *share* of the US recorded-music dollar roughly **doubled from ~34% (2015) to ~82-84% (2024-25)** — a genuinely comparable, basis-robust claim. The *dollar* comparison needs a same-basis caveat as above.
