# SoundExchange — Key Numbers

Retrieved 2026-08-01. PDF saved: `SoundExchange/SoundExchange_2024_Fiscal_Report_Audited.pdf` (official annual report filed under 37 C.F.R. 370.5(c), audited financials).

## What SoundExchange pays
Statutory digital performance royalties (non-interactive/webcasting/satellite radio — e.g. Pandora, SiriusXM — NOT on-demand interactive services like Spotify/Apple Music, which pay artists via labels/distributors instead). Distributed per the Copyright Act allocation formula (17 U.S.C. 114(g)(2)):
- 50% to sound recording copyright owner (usually the label)
- 45% to featured artist(s), pro rata
- 2.5% to non-featured musicians (via AFM-administered escrow)
- 2.5% to non-featured vocalists (via SAG-AFTRA-administered escrow)

## Annual gross distributions (audited, from SoundExchange 2024 Annual Report table, $ millions)
| Year | Total Royalties Collected | Total Gross Distributions | Total Expenses | Admin Rate |
|------|---------------------------|----------------------------|-----------------|------------|
| 2022 | $1,017 | $959 | $74 | 6.6% |
| 2023 | $1,098 | $1,005 | $71 | 4.3% |
| 2024 | $1,056 | $1,054 | $82 | 5.2% |

Footnote from report: statutory royalties specifically (excluding non-statutory/settlement/foreign-society flows) were $936M in 2023 and $909M in 2024.

Cross-check: RIAA's 2024 Year-End Revenue Report lists "SoundExchange Distributions" (within its streaming-revenue table) as $1,004.8M (2023) → $1,054.0M (2024), +4.9% — consistent with SoundExchange's own $1,005M/$1,054M figures. Good independent corroboration.

## More recent / other-year figures (press releases, not yet in an audited annual report)
- 2025 full year (unaudited): ~$991.5M, down 5.9% vs 2024, per Digital Music News coverage citing SoundExchange data — attributed to fewer settlements, lower SiriusXM-reported revenue, and the cumulative effect of a SiriusXM underpayment dispute (SoundExchange sued SiriusXM in 2023).
- Q1 2025: $253.2M; Q2 2025: $241.5M; H1 2025 combined: $494.7M
- 2015: $802.6M (per SoundExchange historical press release), up 4% from $773.4M in 2014
- 2019: $908.2M, down 4.7% YoY (cited as impacted by a prior-year $150M SiriusXM settlement)

## Cumulative distribution milestones
- 2015: >$3B cumulative since 2003 inception
- Late 2017: >$5B cumulative
- ~2020: >$7B cumulative
- ~2023 (per press release): $9B milestone, 150th royalty distribution
- Feb 2025: >$12B cumulative
- By ~mid/late 2025: >$13B cumulative, "more than 800,000 music creators" served (per SoundExchange "TOPS $13B" release)
- Earlier press materials cite "more than 700,000 music creators" (2025 Q2 release) vs "800,000" ($13B release) — membership/payee-count figures appear to have grown across 2025; use the most recent figure and note the vintage.
