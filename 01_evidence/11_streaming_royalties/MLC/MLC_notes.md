# MLC (Mechanical Licensing Collective) — Key Numbers

Retrieved 2026-08-01. PDFs saved: `MLC/MLC_2024_Annual_Report.pdf`, `MLC/MLC_2025_Annual_Report.pdf`.

## Cumulative royalties distributed (mechanical royalties to songwriters/publishers, US)
- Inception: January 2021 (MLC began collecting under the Music Modernization Act blanket license)
- Oct 2023 (3rd annual meeting): ~$1.5B cumulative
- March 2024: ~$2B cumulative
- Oct 2024 (4th annual meeting): ~$2.5B cumulative
- 2024 calendar year: $771.1M in blanket royalties directly distributed for 2024 usage ($716.9M initial processing + $54.2M reprocessing), per monthly distribution table in the 2024 Annual Report (p.~20). Monthly range ran roughly $54.9M–$67.1M.
- End of 2025 (5th annual meeting, Oct 1 2025): >$3B cumulative distributed
- As of March 2026 (per 2025 Annual Report, published after FY2025 close): >$4B cumulative total royalties **processed** (note: report uses "processed" not "distributed" for this headline figure — check definitional consistency before citing)
- 2025 calendar year: $943M+ in total royalties processed for 2025 usage (2025 Annual Report "by the numbers" box)

## Membership
- End of 2024: ~47,000–50,000 members (sources vary slightly: MBW article says 47,000 enrolled as of ~March 2024 update; MLC's own Oct 2024 press release says surpassed 50,000)
- 2024 new members: ~11,000 (MLC 2024 report says 16K+ new members in the "2024 by the numbers" box — reconcile discrepancy between press coverage (11K) and annual report box (16K+) before citing precisely)
- End of 2025: 74,000+ members (MLC 2025 Annual Report, "Five Years of Progress" box)
- 2025 new members: 24,000+ (largest single-year increase to date, per 2025 report)

## Match rate / database
- Match rate ~90% (through Sept 2024 distribution) → ~92% (through Sept 2025 distribution)
- Public database: 42M works (2024) → 50M+ works (Oct 2025) → 52M+ (year-end 2025)

## "Black box" / historical unmatched royalties
- Historical unmatched royalties transferred to The MLC by DSPs at launch (covers usage 2008–2020, i.e. Phono I/II/III rate periods): initial value ~$424–427M; revised down to **$397.2M** (or ~$397,286,419) after CRB finalized Phonorecords III rates and DSPs delivered adjustments in Feb 2024.
- By DSP (of the original historical transfer): Apple Music $148.3M, Spotify $146.3M, Amazon Music $36.6M, Google/YouTube $30.1M, Pandora $15.2M (figures per MLC's Historical Unmatched Royalties dashboard, themlc.com/historical-unmatched-royalties, retrieved 2026-08-01 — dashboard is live/updating, so figures reflect a snapshot).
- Distributed to date (per 2024 Annual Report, "as of June 2025 distribution"): $223.42M distributed of $397.20M transferred = 56.2% overall match rate on historical pool; broken down as Phono 1 ~92.6% matched, Phono 2 ~53.4% matched, Phono 3 ~56.6% matched (numbers approximate — PDF table layout made exact figure-to-label mapping imperfect; verify against dashboard before publishing).
- By Oct 2025 (per MusicRow coverage of 5th annual meeting): $225M+ historical royalties distributed, described as "over 57%" of the unmatched pool matched.
- MLC's own framing: as of ~2025-2026 they describe the black box as "fully illuminated" (i.e., made searchable/transparent) even though ~43-44% of the historical pool remained undistributed as of mid-2025 — "illuminated" refers to transparency/tools, not full distribution.

## Caveats for sidebar use
- MLC royalties are **mechanical** royalties (paid to songwriters/publishers for the reproduction right), distinct from SoundExchange (performance royalties to artists/labels) and from direct label/DSP payouts. Do not sum MLC + SoundExchange totals as if they were both "artist" money — MLC flows to the songwriting/publishing side, which may or may not be the same person as the performing artist.
- "Total royalties distributed" vs "total royalties processed" are used inconsistently across MLC's own communications (2025 report leads with "processed" as the headline metric). Use processed/distributed consistently within any one comparison.
- No Texas- or Austin-specific breakdown of MLC members or royalties is published.
