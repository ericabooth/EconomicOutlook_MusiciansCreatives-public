## Extracted substantive text

**Program description.** The Musical Tax Credit applies to production companies meeting specified
qualified production expenditure thresholds for qualified musical and theatrical performances. The
program was effective for taxable years beginning January 1, 2018 through December 31, 2022.

**Expenditure thresholds** (minimum qualified production expenditures, by project type):

| Project type | Minimum spend |
|---|---|
| Musical or theatrical performance | $500,000 |
| Recorded musical performance incorporated into / synchronized with a movie, television, or interactive entertainment production | $250,000 |
| Other recorded musical performance | $100,000 |

**Annual aggregate caps and per-company maximums:**

| Taxable years beginning | Annual aggregate cap | Per-company maximum |
|---|---|---|
| 1/1/2018 – before 1/1/2019 | $5 million | $1 million |
| 1/1/2019 – before 1/1/2020 | $10 million | $2 million |
| 1/1/2020 – before 1/1/2023 | $15 million | $3 million |

**Process requirements.** Companies must obtain pre-certification from the Georgia Department of
Economic Development (GDEcD) and preapproval from the Department of Revenue via the Georgia
Tax Center.

**Excess credit.** Any excess credit may be used to offset the production company's withholding taxes
(Form IT-WH, Electronic Notice of Intent).

**Authorities cited on the page.** O.C.G.A. § 48-7-40.33; Revenue Regulation 560-7-8-.61.

**Status.** Program sunset after December 31, 2022.
