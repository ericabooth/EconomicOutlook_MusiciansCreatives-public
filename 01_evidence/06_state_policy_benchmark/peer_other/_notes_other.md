## NEW MEXICO

### New Mexico Music Commission (NMMC)
- **Program name:** New Mexico Music Commission; grant program is the
  **"Music in the Community Grant Program."**
- **Authority:** Governor-appointed commission. Administratively housed under the
  **New Mexico Department of Cultural Affairs** — the commission administrator's email is
  `@dca.nm.gov`, and offices are listed at the Bataan Memorial Building, 407 Galisteo St.,
  Suite 270, Santa Fe, NM 87501.
  - **Statutory citation NOT found.** The commission's site is mid-rebuild and carries no
    enabling-statute reference.
- **Mission (verbatim):** "To protect, promote, and preserve the musical traditions of New
  Mexico; to foster appreciation of the value of music; and to encourage the educational,
  creative, and professional musical activities of the residents of New Mexico."
- **Grant purpose (verbatim):** "To provide public funding to support new or existing music
  engagement, music education, music appreciation, or music performance programs or
  activities in New Mexico with an emphasis on serving **rural, tribal, veteran, disability,
  or other underserved communities**."

**Funding level — this is the notable number:**
- **Grant amount: $2,500 flat per award.** No matching funds required. Paid on a
  reimbursement basis after project completion and invoice submission. Funds may be taxable.
- **Total program appropriation: NOT FOUND.** The site does not publish the number of
  awards or the aggregate budget. Without an award count, total program size is unknown.

- **Eligible applicants:** New Mexico-based businesses, nonprofits, tribal and local
  government entities, K-12 schools and higher education institutions, and individuals
  (individuals must name a community partner organization and supply a letter of commitment).
- **Eligible activities:** musical performance in local venues or festivals; lectures or
  presentations on music topics; instrument demonstrations; workshops (performance,
  instrument building/repair, music or lyric writing); exhibitions on music topics;
  artistic, administrative and technical fees; consultant and facilitator fees.
- **Ineligible:** projects or organizations outside New Mexico; investment or contingency funding.

**Status as of 2026-08-01: ENACTED AND OPERATING, BUT CURRENTLY CLOSED TO APPLICATIONS.**
Verbatim: "The 2026/7 Music in the Community Grant program is currently closed. We
anticipate reopening the grant application in **May 2027** for projects occurring between
**July 1 2027 and June 15, 2028**."

**Framing note for the paper:** at $2,500 per award, this is a community-engagement and
cultural-preservation grant, not an industry incentive. It is not comparable in kind or
scale to New York's or Tennessee's programs. Useful mainly as an example of a
**low-cost, governor-appointed state music commission** with an explicit rural/tribal
equity mandate.

## COLORADO

### Colorado Office of Film, Television and Media (COFTM)
- **Authority:** a program office within the **Colorado Office of Economic Development and
  International Trade (OEDIT)**. Statutory citation **not retrieved**.
- **Mission (verbatim):** COFTM "attracts and facilitates content creation in the state to
  generate economic growth. It promotes the state's film and television industry locally,
  nationally, and internationally while highlighting Colorado's unique rural areas."

**Programs listed (verbatim descriptions):**
- **Colorado Film Incentive:** "This incentive encourages film production in Colorado with a
  performance-based **refundable tax** for **up to 22%** of qualified expenses."
- **Film Festival Tax Credit:** "established by **HB 25-1005**, offers incentives to a
  globally recognized film festival to relocate to Colorado, and to existing film festivals
  by providing financial support for qualifying expenditures related to organizing and
  hosting their film festival in Colorado."
- Non-financial supports: Film Festival Support and Promotion Sponsorship; Colorado Film
  Crew and Support Services Directory; Colorado Film Location Directory; Regional Film
  Liaisons; Career Connections Panels for high school and college classrooms.

**Cumulative impact claim (agency self-reported, verbatim):**
> "Colorado's film incentive program has generated **$400 million in estimated economic
> impact** to **58 counties** in Colorado and created over **6,900 cast and crew positions**
> since its inception."
- **Caveat:** no vintage, no methodology, no base year. Agency marketing figure. Do not
  cite as an evaluation result.

**Annual appropriation: NOT FOUND.** Colorado's film incentive has historically been
appropriation-limited, but no dollar figure was retrieved this pass.

**Music coverage: NO MUSIC-SPECIFIC PROGRAM IDENTIFIED.** Nothing in COFTM's program list
addresses music, scoring, recording, or live music.

### Colorado Creative Industries (CCI)
- Also within OEDIT. Mission: "to promote, support and expand the creative industries to
  drive Colorado's economy, grow jobs and enhance our quality of life."
- Defines creative economy subgroups as: **design, film and media, literary and publishing,
  performing arts, visual arts and design, and heritage** — note that **music is not named
  as its own subgroup**; it would fall under performing arts.
- Current strategic plan: **"Arts For All 2030: Colorado Creative Industries Strategic Plan
  2025-2030."**
- Grant programs named on the site: Governor's Creative Leadership Award; Folk and
  Traditional Arts Project Grant; Equity in Arts Learning for Colorado Youth Grant;
  Colorado Community Revitalization Grant; Colorado Community Revitalization Tax Credit;
  Accessibility and Disability Etiquette Training.
- **No music-specific grant program identified. No dollar figures retrieved.**
- Asserted context (unsourced agency claim): "Colorado ranks number 1 in the percentage of
  residents who perform or create artworks, according to the National Endowment for the
  Arts' Participation Study." Vintage not given.

**Live-music venue relief: NOT FOUND.** Colorado ran pandemic-era venue relief, but nothing
current surfaced in this pass and no figures were retrieved.

**Status as of 2026-08-01:** film incentive and CCI grant programs appear **enacted and
operating**; **music-specific state support: none identified.**
