# Texas Music Incubator Rebate (TMIR) Program — Texas Music Office program page

- **Source URL:** https://gov.texas.gov/music/tmir
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-01
- **Publisher:** Texas Music Office, Office of the Governor (Music, Film, Television, and Multimedia Office)
- **Page vintage:** current as of retrieval; states "The 2025 application cycle is currently closed. The 2026 application cycle will open on September 1."

## Statutory authority
Senate Bill 609, 87th Legislature, Regular Session (2021). Signed by Gov. Greg Abbott.
Codified at Government Code ch. 485, Subchapter C (§§ 485.041–485.047).

## Funding
- **$20.2 million biennial funding** (this is the statutory dedication of $10.1M/fiscal year; see SB 609 §§ 2–3 and 2026-27 GAA).
- **FY2026 awards: $10.1 million awarded to 180 recipients across 60 Texas cities.** (Figure as stated on TMO page; corroborated by TMO Facebook post "In FY2026, the Texas Music Incubator Rebate Program awarded $10.1 million to 180 mu[sic venues]…", https://www.facebook.com/texasmusicoffice/posts/1393226669502889/)

## Award structure
- Maximum award: **up to $100,000** per recipient per fiscal year (one rebate per tax ID annually).
- Amount = mixed beverage gross receipts taxes **or** state sales tax on beer and wine remitted in the previous fiscal year, capped at $100,000.

## Eligibility (must hold for at least the two years prior to application)
1. Mixed beverage tax permittee **or** beer/wine sales tax permit holder.
2. Music venues: dedicated audience capacity of **no more than 3,000 people**.
3. Music festivals: held in a **county with population under 100,000**.
4. Written contracts with artists for live performances specifying compensation (percentage of ticket/other sales, or a guaranteed advance).
5. Meet at least **five** of eight operational criteria, one of which must be either (A) marketing live music performances through printed/electronic listings, or (B) providing live music performances **five or more nights per week**.

## Application cycle (standard timeline)
- Applications open: **September 1**
- Deadline: **September 30**
- Review: **October 1 – January 31**
- Determinations: beginning **February 1**

Note: the statute (Gov't Code § 485.044(c)) says applications are accepted "beginning September 1 of each year" with rebates paid until the account is exhausted; it does not set a September 30 close. The 30-day window is an administrative choice by TMO. The **first round** ran September 1 – November 30, 2023 (a longer window).
