## Method notes and limits of this pass

- **Tools:** WebSearch (budget exhausted at 200 calls partway through — several planned searches could not be run), WebFetch, and curl with a desktop browser User-Agent. PDFs text-mined with `pdftotext -layout`.
- **What the search-budget exhaustion cost:** NCSL substitutes, Texas sales-tax exemption research, the FY2025 TMIR round total, and the New Mexico / Colorado / Washington quick adds were all curtailed. These are listed as gaps in `_findings.md`.
- **Terminology discipline applied throughout:** statutory cap ≠ pre-certified ≠ preapproved ≠ certified ≠ allocated ≠ claimed. Each state's notes label which stage a figure represents. Do not build a comparison table that mixes stages without labeling them.
- **Enacted vs. funded** is tracked explicitly for every program. Texas TMIR was enacted 2021 and unfunded until 2023. Georgia's credit was enacted and capped at $15M/year and disbursed $0. Louisiana's 2025 extension passed both chambers and was vetoed. Georgia's proposed music office may be enacted-but-unfunded. None of these read correctly from a statute alone.
