## Program description (FY2026-27 document, verbatim)

> "**330.17 Film and Television Incentive Fund**
>
> The Film and Television Incentive Fund, established by a **2006 law**, provides incentive
> grants to encourage the production of films, movies, television shows, computer-generated
> imagery, interactive digital media, streaming platforms, and **stand-alone post-production
> scoring and editing** in Tennessee. To be eligible, such productions must meet criteria
> established by the **Tennessee Film, Entertainment and Music Commission**."

This is the budget line that funds **both** the film/TV production incentive **and** the
scoring (music) incentive. There is **no separate music line item**.

## Consolidated series and two warnings

| Fiscal year | State appropriation | Total | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2023-24 | $1,794,500 | $2,417,900 | **Actual** |
| FY2024-25 | $7,620,200 | $8,523,300 | **Actual** |
| FY2025-26 | $10,686,500 | $10,735,100 | Estimated |
| FY2026-27 | $28,687,100 | $28,735,700 | Recommended |

**Warning 1 — estimates overstate actuals.** FY2024-25 state appropriations were
*estimated* at **$10,668,300** in the FY2026 budget document; the *actual* reported a year
later was **$7,620,200**. That is **28.6% below** the estimate. Where both exist, cite the actual.

**Warning 2 — the recurring base is much smaller than the headline.** The FY2026-27
recommended total of $28.7M is a base of **$8,735,700** plus a **one-time $20,000,000**.
Tennessee's ongoing annual commitment to this fund is under **$9M**, not $28.7M. The fund
also carries only **3 FTE**.

**Warning 3 — this is film + music combined.** None of these figures can be attributed to
music alone. Isolating the scoring incentive would require a records request to TNECD.
