## Program terms as stated on the live page (2026-08-01)

**Credit rate:** 25% of qualified production expenditures

**Per-production caps:**
- **Level 1** (Broadway / Times Square area facilities): up to **$3,000,000** per production
- **Level 2** (other Manhattan facilities): up to **$350,000** per production; requires a
  minimum **$750,000** production budget and $750,000 in qualified expenditures

**Aggregate program cap:** **$550 million** (increased by $150 million in the FY2027 Budget)

**Program sunset:** **September 30, 2027** (credit period end date)

**Credit eligibility window:** tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2021 but before
January 1, 2028

**Application deadlines:**
- Shows with first paid performance Dec 1, 2025 – Jun 30, 2026: initial application
  requirement waived up front; must be submitted by **June 30, 2026**
- Shows with first paid performance July 1, 2026 onward: initial application required **in advance**
- All initial applications must be submitted before **June 30, 2027**

> **Important:** these terms **supersede** the program guidelines PDF
> (`ESD_Program-Guidelines-NYC-MT-07052024.pdf`, rev. 2024-07-05), which still states a
> $300 million aggregate cap and a September 30, 2025 sunset. ESD had not reissued the
> guidelines to match the statute as of 2026-08-01.

## Key eligibility and condition terms (from the guidelines PDF, still operative)

- Must be a **for-profit**, live, scripted dramatic performance with or without musical
  accompaniment, performed in a Level 1 or Level 2 qualified NYC production facility.
- **Excluded:** ballet, opera, musical solo, group, band, or orchestra performance;
  solo/duo/several-performer stand-up comedy.
- Facility tests: live theatrical productions must be ≥75% of the facility's gross receipts.
  Level 1 = Manhattan, bounded by and including 41st–54th Streets and 6th–9th Avenues,
  500+ seats. Level 2 = Manhattan, 100+ seats.
- The **credit period** begins on the production start date (up to 12 weeks before first
  performance) and ends at the earliest of: reaching the production's credit cap, the
  program sunset date, or the production's close.
- Initial application must include:
  1. a **diversity and arts job training program plan** — paid, multi-year training,
     mentoring and networking for fellows. Pre-approved programs: **The Theatre Leadership
     Project** and the **Black Theatre Coalition Fellowship Program**. Self-operated
     fellowships require advance approval.
  2. a **public access and availability plan** for low- or no-cost access for low-income New Yorkers.
  3. an **attestation** to comply with the NYSCA cultural program fund contribution
     requirement — up to **50% of total credits received**, payable from 25% of net
     operating profits monthly, triggered if the production earns ongoing revenue
     prospectively of at least **200% of its ongoing production costs**. Funds are
     earmarked for arts education and arts/cultural programs.
