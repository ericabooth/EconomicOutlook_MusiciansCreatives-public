# Louisiana Entertainment — music program pages (administering agency)

Two program pages from Louisiana Entertainment, the entertainment-industry brand of the Louisiana
Department of Economic Development's Office of Entertainment Industry Development (OEID).

---

## 1. Sound Recording Program

- **URL:** https://www.louisianaentertainment.gov/music/sound-recording-program
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-01
- **Publisher:** Louisiana Economic Development, Office of Entertainment Industry Development

### Extracted substantive text

**Program description.** The Sound Recording Incentive Program provides an 18 percent tax credit for
sound recording projects — music, poetry, spoken-word — made and produced in Louisiana. Credits are
issued as **rebates**.

**Statutory citation.** Officially the Sound Recording Investor Tax Credit. Statute at legis.la.gov
(Law document id 321887 = R.S. 47:6023). Rules at Louisiana Administrative Code, Part 1, Chapter 65.

**Rate and caps.**
- 18 percent credit on eligible production expenditures
- Annual program cap: $2.16 million
- Per-project cap: $100,000 per year

**Base investment minimums.**
- General: $25,000 minimum
- Louisiana residents: $10,000 minimum

**Administering agency.** Office of Entertainment Industry Development (OEID), Louisiana Department
of Economic Development. Rebate checks issued by the Louisiana Department of Revenue.

**Application process.** (1) Submit completed application and fee; (2) LED issues Initial Certification if
eligible; (3) track production expenses; (4) submit cost report with expenditure verification deposit;
(5) independent CPA reviews expenses; (6) LED issues Final Certification; (7) LDR issues rebate check.

**Sunset notice (quoted from page).** "The program sunsets on 6/30/25. No new applications will be
accepted after 6/30/25."

**Contact.** Amanda Hafford, Division Director — amanda.hafford@la.gov; 225.342.5633

## 2. Live Performance Production Program

- **URL:** https://www.louisianaentertainment.gov/live/live-performance-production-program
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-01
- **Publisher:** Louisiana Economic Development, Office of Entertainment Industry Development

### Extracted substantive text

**Program status notice (quoted from page).** "The Live Performance, Sound Recording and Qualified
Music Company programs sunset on 6/30/25. No new applications for these three programs will be
accepted after 6/30/25."

**Program description.** The Live Performance Production Incentive Program — statutorily the Musical
and Theatrical Production Income Tax Credit — supports concert tours, theatrical productions, and live
performance events that originate or debut in Louisiana. Eligible productions include pre-Broadway
engagements, touring company debuts, resident productions, and concert tours launching their first
public performance in the state.

**Credit rates by expenditure tier.**

| Certified Louisiana expenditures | Credit rate |
|---|---|
| $100,000 – $300,000 | 7% |
| $300,000 – $1,000,000 | 14% |
| Over $1,000,000 | 18% |
| Payroll to Louisiana residents | additional 7% |

**Caps.**
- Annual program cap: $10 million, with $5 million reserved for nonprofit productions
- Per-project annual cap: $1 million

**Minimum in-state expenditure.** $100,000

**Administering agency.** Louisiana Economic Development (LED), Office of Entertainment Industry
Development (OEID).

**Application process.** Submit application with supporting documents and fees; pay expenditure
verification report deposit ($2,500–$5,000 depending on production size); receive Initial Certification;
track expenses; submit cost report for CPA review; receive Final Certification confirming the credit
amount; claim refund or transfer credits (one-time, transferable).

**Contact.** Amanda Hafford, Division Director — amanda.hafford@la.gov; 225.342.5633

---

## Caveats

- Neither page publishes certified-credit totals by year, project counts, or cap-utilization figures.
- Both pages describe programs that are closed to new applications. Credits may still be earned and
  claimed on applications submitted before the June 30, 2025 cutoff, so claimed amounts will continue
  to appear in Louisiana fiscal reporting for several more years.
