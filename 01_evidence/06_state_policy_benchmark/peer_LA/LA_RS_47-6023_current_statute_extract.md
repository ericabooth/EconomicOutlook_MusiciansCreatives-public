# La. R.S. 47:6023 — Sound Recording Investor Tax Credit (current statute extract)

- **URL:** https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/Law.aspx?d=321887
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-01
- **Publisher:** Louisiana State Legislature (official)
- **Vintage:** current law as posted; reflects amendments through the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session

---

## Extracted provisions

**Credit rates.**
- Project-based production credit, for applications received on or after July 1, 2017: **18 percent** of
  base investment
- Company-based Qualified Music Company (QMC) payroll credit, Tier 1: **10 percent** for each new job
  with QMC payroll of $35,000–$66,000 per year
- Company-based QMC payroll credit, Tier 2: **15 percent** for each new job with QMC payroll of
  $66,000–$200,000 per year

**Base investment minimums.**
- Project-based, general: base investment in excess of **$25,000**
- Project-based, Louisiana resident investor with a resident copyright: base investment in excess of
  **$10,000**
- QMC: minimum three new jobs at the Tier 1 wage level

**Annual aggregate cap and reserve.** Aggregate credits certified for all investors in any calendar year
shall not exceed **$2,160,000**. "fifty percent of the aggregate amount of credits certified each year
shall be reserved for QMCs."

**Per-project cap.** "No more than one hundred thousand dollars in tax credits may be granted per
project, per calendar year."

**Allocation.** First-come, first-served, on application for initial certification to Louisiana Economic
Development.

**Administering agency.** Louisiana Economic Development, in consultation with the Department of
Revenue.

**Sunset (quoted exactly).**

> "No credits shall be granted pursuant to the provisions of this Section for applications received on
> or after July 1, 2025."

**Amending Acts listed in the statute's source note.** Acts 2005, No. 485; Acts 2007, No. 368;
Acts 2009, No. 475; Acts 2013, No. 385; Acts 2015, Nos. 125, 357, 412; Acts 2016 (1st Ex. Sess.),
No. 29; Acts 2017, Nos. 275, 323, 400; Acts 2019, No. 363; Acts 2021, No. 401; Acts 2024
(3rd Ex. Sess.), Nos. 5 and 11.

---

## Caveat

The statute as posted still carries the 18 percent rate and the July 1, 2025 application cutoff. House
Bill 653 of the 2025 Regular Session would have rewritten these provisions but was **vetoed** — see
`_notes_LA.md`, section 5. Read the posted text as operative law.
