# HAAM seeks city help to offset expected $1M shortfall

- **Title:** HAAM seeks city help to offset expected $1M shortfall
- **Outlet:** Austin Monitor
- **Author:** Chad Swiatecki
- **Publication date:** 2020-06-08 (article notes an update dated 2020-06-07)
- **URL:** https://www.austinmonitor.com/stories/2020/06/haam-seeks-city-help-to-offset-expected-1m-shortfall/
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

> The organization projects a "$1 million shortfall by the end of 2020," which could force the nonprofit to reduce healthcare services to approximately 1,000 local musicians.

> HAAM currently serves over 2,600 members.

> Executive Director Reenie Collins told city leaders that "decreased donations and increased costs for health care and insurance plans" are driving the shortfall.

> The newly created ANCHOR fund ($6 million) initially prevented membership organizations from accessing relief funds. City Council members Kathie Tovo and Alison Alter worked to clarify that organizations like HAAM providing direct services should qualify, distinguishing them from traditional grant-making foundations.

> Music Commission member Graham Reynolds suggested considering a second phase of the Austin Music Disaster Relief Fund (first round: $1.5 million in $1,000 individual musician grants) that could support essential music industry organizations.

## Why it matters

Quantifies the safety-net side of the Austin musician economy: HAAM served 2,600+ members, faced a $1M shortfall in 2020, and risked cutting services to ~1,000 musicians. Also confirms the Austin Music Disaster Relief Fund grant size ($1,000 per musician) and the $6 million ANCHOR fund.
