# Index: Austin musician pay and livelihoods in journalism, 2014-2021

- **Beat:** Journalism roughly 2014 through 2021 on Austin musician pay and livelihoods
- **Compiled:** 2026-08-01
- **Articles saved:** 19
- **Outlets represented:** Austin Monitor (7), The Austin Chronicle (6), KUT (4), CultureMap Austin (1), Community Impact (1)

All files follow the pattern `news_YYYY-MM-DD_outlet_short-slug.md`. Every file records the URL and a 2026-08-01 retrieval date. Excerpts preserve numbers as published.

**Scope note.** This index covers only the 19 articles dated 2015-2021 listed below. The folder also contains `news_*` files dated 2022 and later, plus PDFs and IRS filings, contributed by other collection efforts. Those are outside this beat and are not indexed here.

## 1. Census release and the "booming industry, broke musicians" framing (2015-2016)

| Date | Outlet | Title | Key number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2015-06-01 | KUT | [One-Fifth of Austin's Musicians Live Below Federal Poverty Level, Census Says](https://www.kut.org/austin/2015-06-01/one-fifth-of-austins-musicians-live-below-federal-poverty-level-census-says) | 20% of Austin musicians below the federal poverty level; 2,300+ musicians surveyed |
| 2015-06-01 | Austin Monitor | [Austin Music Census reveals fault lines in industry](https://austinmonitor.com/stories/2015/06/austin-music-census-reveals-fault-lines-in-industry/) | ~50% of musicians qualify for federal housing assistance; permitting touches a minimum of 7 city departments |
| 2015-06-04 | The Austin Chronicle | [Playback: Austin Music Census](https://www.austinchronicle.com/music/2015-06-05/playback-austin-music-census/) | 1,288 of 1,883 musician respondents earned under $10,000 from music in 2013; 87% call pay "stagnated"; $45,000 census budget; 3,968 total respondents |
| 2015-08-25 | KUT | [The Rising Costs & Gender Disparity Within Austin's Music Scene](https://www.kut.org/austin/2015-08-25/the-rising-costs-gender-disparity-within-austins-music-scene) | Only 20% of 2,000+ surveyed musicians were women; rents up 50% since 2001 |
| 2016-02-22 | KUT | [Report: Austin Music Is Now A $1.8-Billion Industry, But The Local Scene Is Struggling](https://www.kut.org/post/report-austin-music-now-18-billion-industry-local-scene-struggling) | $1.6B (2010) to $1.8B (2014); tourism music jobs +3,780 (+37%), non-tourism music jobs -1,205 (-15%) |

One-line summaries:
- **KUT 2015-06-01** — Same-day report on the census release; establishes the poverty headline and the survey base.
- **Austin Monitor 2015-06-01** — Policy-desk read of the census, linking musician income to permitting, sound ordinance enforcement, and Red River.
- **Chronicle 2015-06-04** — Most numerically detailed contemporaneous account, including census cost, sample sizes, and the income distribution.
- **KUT 2015-08-25** — Only piece in the set with the census gender breakdown, plus the long-run rent trend.
- **KUT 2016-02-22** — The clean statement of the paradox: total impact rises while local (non-tourism) music employment falls.

## 2. Affordability, exodus, and the venue crisis (2015-2018)

| Date | Outlet | Title | Key number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2015-12-23 | CultureMap Austin | [Looking back at Austin's biggest music venue and club closings of 2015](https://austin.culturemap.com/news/entertainment/12-23-15-bar-music-venue-closings-closures-list-austin-trend-2015/) | 6 venues closed in 2015; 3 of them in Red River, all citing rising rent; Austin Music Hall demolished for a 28-story office building |
| 2016-05-26 | The Austin Chronicle | [We Can't Make It Here Anymore](https://www.austinchronicle.com/music/2016-05-27/we-cant-make-it-here-anymore/) | Local artist/business/venue share of the $1.8B economy down more than 15%; 72 EDD proposals narrowed to 12 Music Commission priorities |
| 2017-01-26 | The Austin Chronicle | [Can a Working Musician Find a Decent Job in This Town?](https://www.austinchronicle.com/music/2017-01-27/can-a-working-musician-find-a-decent-job-in-this-town/) | 56% hold non-music jobs; 20% below the $11,670 poverty level; ~40% of full-time musicians earn $15,000 or less |
| 2017-04-21 | Austin Monitor | [Study: Red River music venues face '11th hour' endangerment](https://www.austinmonitor.com/stories/2017/04/study-red-river-music-venues-face-11th-hour-endangerment/) | 13 Red River venues studied; 20 recommended solutions including "agent of change" |
| 2018-07-16 | Austin Monitor | [Another report, same story: Austin's too pricey for artists](https://www.austinmonitor.com/stories/2018/07/another-report-same-story-austins-too-pricey-for-artists/) | 2017 Creative Space Survey of 500+ artists: 42% considered relocating; 23% on month-to-month leases; 12% face imminent displacement |

One-line summaries:
- **CultureMap 2015-12-23** — Year-end closure tally showing rent as the recurring cause, concentrated in Red River.
- **Chronicle 2016-05-26** — The definitive long-form treatment of the affordability crossroads; documents the exodus to Lockhart and the full city policy chain.
- **Chronicle 2017-01-26** — Best source for the day-job dimension; restates census income distribution against MSA mean and median wages.
- **Austin Monitor 2017-04-21** — Urban Land Institute panel declares the "11th hour" for Red River and puts "agent of change" on the policy menu.
- **Austin Monitor 2018-07-16** — Second independent survey confirms displacement, and records artist frustration at three years of studies without delivery.

## Figures flagged for verification before use

These circulated in search-result summaries during collection but were **not** confirmed verbatim in any article text retrieved here. Verify against the Austin Music Census report itself before citing.

- Austin musician average total income **$23,371/year**, of which **$15,504** from creative work; **81%** hold side jobs.
- Implied affordable rent of about **$388/month** for music-only income, against reported average musician rent of **$749/month**.
- Per-gig pay of roughly **$50-$100 per musician**, described as unchanged for 40-plus years. The best-sourced version of this claim appears in a KUT story dated 2022-02-15 ("Same as it ever was"), which falls outside this beat's window.
- The **$15,475** median income figure named in the assignment did not appear in any article retrieved. The closest confirmed figures are the Chronicle's "1,288 of 1,883 earned under $10,000" and the "nearly 40% of full-time musicians earn $15,000 or less" restatement.

## Cross-source conflicts noted in the files

- **Venue closure risk, July 2020.** Austin Monitor headlines 90%; KUT reports 60%+ as the survey finding with 90% attributed to a single respondent.
- **Hotel tax and venues, Sept 2019.** Community Impact reports hotel tax revenue "cannot be used on music venues"; Austin Monitor describes the allocation as a first for direct funding to musicians *and for-profit venues*.
