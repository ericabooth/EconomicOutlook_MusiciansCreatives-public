## Why it matters

**The most current documentation of the City of Austin's official musician pay rate schedule** (as of mid-2026), and the clearest statement of the current Live Music Fund grant architecture. This is the endpoint of the $150 → $200 policy rate trajectory that began in 2016.

## City of Austin musician pay rate (current, 2026)

> Applicants need not have paid musicians at the city pay rate — **$200 per musician for groups up to six; $150 for 7-10 people; $1,500 for 10+** — to remain competitive.

Note the structure: a per-musician rate for small groups, a lower per-musician rate for mid-size groups, and a flat ensemble rate for large groups. **Confirm the time unit (per set / per hour / per performance) against a City source** — the Chronicle does not state it, and the 2022 KUT reporting had it as per musician per hour.

## Live Music Fund grant architecture (2026 cycle)

- **Total funding available: $6 million**
- Grant amounts: **$5,000 and $20,000** for individual musicians and promoters
- The $20,000 grant requires an additional "Music Industry Accomplishments" section
- **Application deadline: August 18**
- $20,000 recipients cannot apply in the following cycle; **$5,000 recipients may reapply**
- The same application confers eligibility for **Elevate ($15,000–$30,000)** and **Nexus ($5,000–$10,000)** grants
- Scoring zero on ADA accessibility questions does not disqualify an applicant

## Prior-cycle grantee demographics

- **35%** of recipients identified as LGBTQIA+
- **8%** identified as part of the disability community
