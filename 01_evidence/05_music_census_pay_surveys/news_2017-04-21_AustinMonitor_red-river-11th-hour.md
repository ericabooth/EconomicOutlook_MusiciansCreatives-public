# Study: Red River music venues face '11th hour' endangerment

- **Title:** Study: Red River music venues face '11th hour' endangerment
- **Outlet:** Austin Monitor
- **Author:** Chad Swiatecki
- **Publication date:** 2017-04-21
- **URL:** https://www.austinmonitor.com/stories/2017/04/study-red-river-music-venues-face-11th-hour-endangerment/
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

> The Urban Land Institute completed a study examining the Red River Cultural District's 13 music venues between Sixth and 12th streets.

> "Austin is in the effective '11th hour' of the endangerment of the live music scene."

> The study identifies three primary challenges: development pressures, safety concerns, and minimal profit margins for venue operators. Venues mentioned include Mohawk, Beerland, and Cheer Up Charlies.

> The report recommends 20 solutions spanning short-, mid-, and long-term horizons, including: citywide music venue preservation funding mechanisms; extended outdoor music hours on trial basis; implementation of "agent of change" noise policies; enhanced district safety measures; public-private preservation agreements and potential strike funds; Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone funding exploration; streetscape improvements.

> The study was commissioned by the Red River Cultural District Merchants Association and examined data collected in late 2016.

## Why it matters

Documents the Urban Land Institute technical assistance panel finding on the Red River district (13 venues, data collected late 2016) and is the clearest news record of the "agent of change" noise policy entering Austin's formal policy menu.
