# Looking back at Austin's biggest music venue and club closings of 2015

- **Title:** Looking back at Austin's biggest music venue and club closings of 2015
- **Outlet:** CultureMap Austin
- **Author:** Nicole Raney
- **Publication date:** 2015-12-23 (12:59 pm)
- **URL:** https://austin.culturemap.com/news/entertainment/12-23-15-bar-music-venue-closings-closures-list-austin-trend-2015/
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

Venues closed in 2015, as listed in the article:

> Holy Mountain (Red River Cultural District) — closed due to rent increase
> Red 7 (Red River Cultural District) — closed due to rising rent
> Red Eyed Fly (Red River Cultural District) — closed due to rising rent
> Lanai (Downtown) — closed March 2015
> Dallas Nightclub (North Austin/Burnet Road) — closed September 2015; operated for "more than three decades"
> Trailer Space Records (East Austin) — operated as music venue for "about eight years"

> Austin Music Hall: demolished early 2016 (to become a 28-story office building).

> Hole in the Wall renewed a 5-year lease; the venue was 41 years old as of 2015.

> "actively searching for a smaller, higher quality music venue to open under the [Austin Music Hall] name."

## Why it matters

Year-end tally documenting the 2015 venue-closure wave concentrated in the Red River Cultural District, with rent increases named as the recurring cause. Provides the venue-side counterpart to the census's musician-income findings and dates the Austin Music Hall demolition.
