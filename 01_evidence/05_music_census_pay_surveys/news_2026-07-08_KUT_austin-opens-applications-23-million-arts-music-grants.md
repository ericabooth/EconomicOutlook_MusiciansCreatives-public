## Why it matters

The most current (mid-2026) snapshot of the full City of Austin cultural funding stack and its scale. Confirms the Live Music Fund at **$6 million** within a **$23 million** total, all Hotel Occupancy Tax funded — reinforcing that Austin's musician support is structurally tourism-financed.

## Key figures

- **Total funding: $23 million** in grants
- **Individual grant range: $5,000 to $250,000**, depending on program
- Award duration: one or two years, funding beginning in **January**
- Application deadline: **August 18 at 6:59 p.m.**

**Four programs:**

| Program | Amount | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Live Music Fund | **$6 million** | musicians, venues, promoters |
| Elevate | **$11 million** | artists, cultural organizations, art producers |
| Heritage Preservation | **$3 million** | groups preserving historic spaces |
| Thrive | **$3 million** | nonprofits reflecting Austin's culture |

## Key excerpt

> "These HOT-funded grants are not just awards, they are a reinvestment of visitor dollars back into the artists, musicians, cultural organizations and heritage stewards who make Austin a sought-out destination." —Angela Means, director, Austin Arts, Culture, Music and Entertainment (ACME)

> Eligible uses: "creative production, public programming, operational and administrative needs, cultural events, media and digital arts projects, dance and movement-based work, literary arts, and more."

$3.5M (2023) → $4.5M (2024) → ~$7M live-music earmark (2025) → **$6M (2026)**. Note the 2025 figure came from a different reporting frame ("~$7 million earmarked for live music"); reconcile the 2025 and 2026 definitions before charting a series.
