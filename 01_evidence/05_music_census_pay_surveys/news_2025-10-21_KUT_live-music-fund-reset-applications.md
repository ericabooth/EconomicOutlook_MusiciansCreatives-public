## Why it matters

**The single best round-by-round record of Live Music Fund grantmaking.** It documents a real policy tradeoff the City ran into: raising grant sizes in 2024 cut the number of recipients from 368 to 137 while applications rose above 1,000, producing an acceptance-rate collapse. The 2025 "reset" reduced maximum award size to spread money wider.

## Round-by-round record

| Round | Total | Artist grant sizes | Venue grant sizes | Recipients | Applicants |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2023 (first)** | $3.5 million | $5,000–$10,000 | — | **368** | 660 |
| **2024 (second)** | $4.5 million | $15,000–$30,000 | $30,000–$60,000 | **137** | 1,000+ |
| **2025 (third, "reset")** | ~$7 million for live music (part of $25M+ across city programs) | $5,000 and $20,000 | $20,000 and $40,000 | — | — |

Approximate acceptance rates: **56% in 2023** (368/660) versus **under 14% in 2024** (137/1,000+).

## Key excerpts

> Manager **Erica Shamaly** on the adjustment: "instead of $30,000, it'll be $20,000 [for individual artists]," with an added $5,000 tier.

> The shift aimed to achieve balance after the second round's low acceptance rate disappointed applicants despite increased funding.
