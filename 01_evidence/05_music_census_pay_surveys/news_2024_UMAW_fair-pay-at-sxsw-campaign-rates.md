- **Title:** Fair Pay at SXSW

## Why it matters

The single best consolidated **time series of SXSW artist pay**, and the source of the pay-to-play arithmetic (application fees collected vastly exceed artist payouts). Because UMAW is a party to the dispute, the fee/payout estimate should be labeled as UMAW's estimate, not an audited figure.

## SXSW artist pay rate history (as stated by UMAW)

| Period | Solo / duo | Band |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-2023 (unchanged "for at least a decade") | $100 | $250 |
| 2023 (announced June 2023, effective SXSW 2024) | $150 | $350 |
| 2024 (effective SXSW 2025) | $175 | $400 |

- **Application fee (2024): $75** (up from the $55 reported by NPR in March 2023).

## Campaign demands (verbatim list)

> 1. Increase showcase compensation to minimum **$750** for all performers
> 2. Include festival wristband with financial compensation (end the choice between payment or attendance)
> 3. Provide identical compensation + wristband to both international and domestic US artists
> 4. Eliminate application fees
> 5. Permanently end defense industry involvement at SXSW

## Pay-to-play arithmetic (UMAW estimate, 2024)

> UMAW estimates SXSW collected approximately **$385,495 in application fees** while paying out roughly **$57,820 total to artists**.

That is a ratio of roughly **6.7 to 1** in fees collected versus artist payments. **Caveat:** this is UMAW's own estimate; the underlying application count and payout basis are not disclosed on the page. Do not present as verified.

## Political context

> Endorsements from the **Austin Parks and Recreation Board** and the **Austin Music Commission** (2023) supporting fair wage ordinances, "though the City Council has not voted on these recommendations."

Demand #5 links this pay campaign to the **2024 SXSW boycott over defense-industry / U.S. Army sponsorship** — the two disputes ran together in the 2024 cycle.
