# Survey Finds Majority Of Austin's Live Music Venues Could Close For Good

- **Title:** Survey Finds Majority Of Austin's Live Music Venues Could Close For Good
- **Outlet:** KUT 90.5 (Texas Standard)
- **Author:** Shelly Brisbin
- **Publication date:** 2020-07-10 (2:24 PM CDT)
- **URL:** https://www.kut.org/austin/2020-07-10/survey-finds-majority-of-austins-live-music-venues-could-close-for-good
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

> 60%+ of Austin music venues face permanent closure risk if forced to remain closed for just four months.

> One respondent estimated potential losses at 90%.

> At least 5 well-known venues had already closed permanently by publication date.

> Federal CARES Act grants provide approximately $40,000 per business [while] many music venues spend that amount monthly on overhead expenses alone.

> "Halloween is the dire mile marker for whether many of our music venues will close and go away for good," according to Chad Swiatecki, who reported on the survey for the Austin Monitor.

> Conducted by the University of Houston's Hobby School of Public Affairs and commissioned by the Austin Chamber of Commerce [in June 2020].

## Why it matters

Important methodological corrective to the widely repeated "90% of Austin venues will close" headline: KUT reports the survey's own finding as 60%+ at risk on a four-month horizon, with 90% coming from one respondent's estimate. Use this file alongside the Austin Monitor version when citing the figure.
