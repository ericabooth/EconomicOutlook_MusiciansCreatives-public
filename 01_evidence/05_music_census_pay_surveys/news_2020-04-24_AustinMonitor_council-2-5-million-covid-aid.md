# Council approves $2.5 million in aid to musicians, artists impacted by Covid-19

- **Title:** Council approves $2.5 million in aid to musicians, artists impacted by Covid-19
- **Outlet:** Austin Monitor
- **Author:** Chad Swiatecki
- **Publication date:** 2020-04-24
- **URL:** https://www.austinmonitor.com/stories/2020/04/council-approves-2-5-million-in-aid-to-musicians-artists-impacted-by-covid-19/
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

> The Austin City Council approved $2.5 million in Covid-19 relief for creative professionals through two separate actions.

> Music Relief ($1.5 million): An ordinance directed funds from emergency budget reserves to the Austin Music Disaster Relief Fund, which provides grants to local musicians based on criteria established by the Music Commission and nonprofit partners.

> Artist Relief ($1 million): A resolution reconfigured the existing Creative Space Assistance Fund into the Austin Artist Disaster Relief Fund to support hard-hit artists experiencing income losses.

> City staff chose emergency reserves over the $3.5 million Live Music Fund because "the hotel tax money has stricter state guidelines on how it can be used," and officials anticipated federal reimbursement for pandemic-related expenses would make emergency funds more flexible.

> Mayor Steve Adler emphasized: "This is one of the groups that's hardest-hit by what's happening here" following the cancellation of South by Southwest.

## Why it matters

Dates and sizes the first municipal COVID relief for Austin musicians ($1.5M music + $1.0M artists = $2.5M), and documents the fiscal-mechanism constraint that hotel occupancy tax dollars in the $3.5M Live Music Fund could not be flexibly deployed. Useful for the policy-instrument section of the paper.
