## Why it matters

Useful mainly for two things the news coverage omits: the **geographic scope definition** (five counties) and the **creative-services spending figures**. Note the framing is notably more upbeat than the Chronicle's or KUT's read of the same report — a good illustration of how the same census supports both "growing" and "squeezed" narratives.

## Key figures

- **Average annual creative services spending: $10,500**, of which **60% ($6,300) is spent locally**.
- Over **50 community engagement partners** involved in fielding.
- Geographic scope: respondents living or working in **Travis, Williamson, Hays, Caldwell, and Bastrop counties** — musicians, venue owners, music nonprofits, and business professionals.
- Fastest-growing areas for the music ecosystem: **San Marcos, Pflugerville, Manor, Buda, Bastrop, Round Rock, Kyle**.
- Full report at **austinmusiccensus.org**.

## Key excerpts

> "Music creatives are increasing" in the ecosystem.

> Housing costs are "shrinking the ecosystem and pushing it outside central Austin."

> "On the survey's new DEI measures, the community is more positive than negative."

## Caution

The DEI characterization here ("more positive than negative") is in tension with the Austin Chronicle's read of the same 14 DEI measures ("concerns over ageism, sexism, and elitism are the clearest concerns" and minority respondents rating treatment more harshly). Source both against the report itself; do not cite either secondhand characterization alone.
