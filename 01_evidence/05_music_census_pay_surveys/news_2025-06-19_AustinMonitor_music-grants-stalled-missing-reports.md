# Dozens of city music grants stalled over missing final reports

- **Title:** Dozens of city music grants stalled over missing final reports
- **Outlet:** Austin Monitor
- **Author:** Chad Swiatecki
- **Publication date:** 2025-06-19
- **URL:** https://austinmonitor.com/stories/2025/06/dozens-of-city-music-grants-stalled-over-missing-final-reports/
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01
- **Access:** Retrieved via WebFetch

## Why it matters

Documents an administrative-burden failure mode that is directly relevant to any policy recommendation about grant-based musician support: money is appropriated, awarded, and then **not delivered** because low-income independent workers cannot navigate municipal contracting. This is a design problem, not a demand problem.

## Key figures

- **60** Live Music Fund grant recipients in non-compliance with reporting requirements.
- **70** total non-compliant grant contracts across all city arts programs.
- Non-compliant contracts hold **$847,500 in unpaid funds** collectively.
- **$550,000** of those unpaid funds are Live Music Fund dollars.
- An additional **$100,000** in grant awards was **forfeited**, of which **$70,000** was Live Music Fund.
- The Live Music Fund was in its **third year** of grantmaking.

## Key excerpts

> Staff attributed compliance challenges to recipients being "first-time grantees unfamiliar with municipal contracting processes."

> "We've heard from many musicians that they don't regularly check email." —Morgan Messick, assistant director, ACME (Austin Center for Events / Arts, Culture, Music and Entertainment), on exploring text reminders instead

> Non-compliant grantees face a three-step delinquency protocol and eventual contract termination, placing them in "bad standing" and barring them from future funding for up to five years.

> Clawbacks of already-paid funds are rare — only three instances in two decades.

## Why the numbers matter

$550,000 in undelivered Live Music Fund money is roughly **12% of a single year's fund collections** ($4.445M in FY2024). At the $5,000 individual grant level, that is on the order of 110 musician-grants not reaching musicians.
