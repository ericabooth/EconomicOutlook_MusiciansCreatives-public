# One-Fifth of Austin's Musicians Live Below Federal Poverty Level, Census Says

- **Title:** One-Fifth of Austin's Musicians Live Below Federal Poverty Level, Census Says
- **Outlet:** KUT 90.5 (KUT Radio, Austin's NPR Station)
- **Author:** Not stated in the article as retrieved
- **Publication date:** 2015-06-01 (4:16 PM CDT)
- **URL:** https://www.kut.org/austin/2015-06-01/one-fifth-of-austins-musicians-live-below-federal-poverty-level-census-says
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

> 20% of Austin musicians live below the federal poverty level.

> More than 50% [of musicians] need part- or full-time work beyond music performance.

> Nearly 50% reported making "little or no money" from live music performances.

> 23% of musicians reported decreased income over the past three years.

> 18% of venue owners reported decreased income over the past three years.

> The City of Austin released findings from its first comprehensive music industry census, surveying over 2,300 local musicians.

> Venues increasingly host private events (corporate gatherings, weddings) rather than local music shows, pushing performances to less desirable nights like Tuesdays instead of Fridays or Saturdays. Audiences show reluctance to pay cover charges for local music.

> Don Pitts, city music division manager, noted: "I think it's common knowledge that it's really hard to make money as a musician" but seeing it quantified "brings the emotions back in."

## Why it matters

Primary same-day news account of the 2014 Austin Music Census release; supplies the headline poverty statistic (20% below federal poverty level) and the survey base (2,300+ musicians) that anchors the "booming industry, broke musicians" framing.
