# The Rising Costs & Gender Disparity Within Austin's Music Scene

- **Title:** The Rising Costs & Gender Disparity Within Austin's Music Scene
- **Outlet:** KUT 90.5
- **Author:** Jasmine Garsd
- **Publication date:** 2015-08-25 (10:04 AM CDT)
- **URL:** https://www.kut.org/austin/2015-08-25/the-rising-costs-gender-disparity-within-austins-music-scene
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

Gender composition:

> Male performers outnumbered female counterparts 4 to 1 on stage.

> Of 2,000+ musicians surveyed, only 20 percent were women.

> Behind-the-scenes: 55 percent male, 45 percent female.

> "On the business side it's almost half and half ... that really strong gender majority male is about musicians, it's not about the industry" – Nikki Rowling, Titan Music Group president

Cost pressure:

> Rent prices increased 50 percent since 2001.

> More than 20 percent of Austin musicians live below the federal poverty line.

> Fewer venues charge cover fees.

> "there are a number of layers to what eventually trickles down to become musician rent affordability" – Rowling

## Why it matters

Only piece in this set with a gender breakdown from the census: 20% of the 2,000+ surveyed performing musicians were women (4-to-1 male-to-female on stage), against a near-even 55/45 split on the business side. Also supplies the 50%-since-2001 rent increase figure and quotes the census author directly.
