## Why it matters

Documents the Live Music Fund's size, its long non-distribution, and the proposed grant architecture. Also a second, independent appearance of a **$150/hour** figure — here as a *proposed* venue-payment rate inside the grant framework, again hourly, and again a policy rate rather than an observed market rate.

## Key figures

- **Live Music Fund total: $3 million**, accumulated since the fund's creation in 2019. As of publication, **no funds had been distributed.**
- **Proposed grant range: $5,000–$10,000 per applicant.**
- **Proposed venue payment rate: $150/hour base, maximum $3,000 per band.**
- Historic musician pay characterized as **~$100 per musician per gig, unchanged for 40+ years.**
- Prior COVID-era relief programs: **Austin Music Disaster Fund, $1,000 grants (June 2020)**; **Creative Worker Relief Grant, $1,000 grants (August 2020).**
- Framework prioritized **P.I.E. — Preservation, Innovation, Elevation** — of historically underrepresented groups. Initial recipients: musicians and independent promoters; venues eligible in later years.

## Key excerpts

> "Austin prides itself and markets itself as the Live Music Capital of the World … and yet the very people who power the engine of the industry … have not been supported in meaningful and impactful ways – namely financially." —Megz Kelli, of Magna Carda
