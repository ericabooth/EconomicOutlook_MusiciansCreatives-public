# Playback: Austin Music Census

- **Title:** Playback: Austin Music Census
- **Outlet:** The Austin Chronicle
- **Author:** Kevin Curtin
- **Publication date:** 2015-06-04 (issue dated 2015-06-05)
- **URL:** https://www.austinchronicle.com/music/2015-06-05/playback-austin-music-census/
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-08-01

## Key excerpts

Survey scope and cost:

> The ATX Music Census and Needs Assessment Survey [was] a $45,000 initiative commissioned by Don Pitts of the city's Music & Entertainment Division. Local consultants Titan Music Group collected data from 3,968 musicians, industry workers, and venue owners.

Musician economics:

> Of 1,883 musician respondents, 1,288 earned less than $10,000 from music in 2013.

> Approximately 75% of local musicians fall below Austin's $48,150 average annual wage.

> Only 0.4% earned over $150,000 from music alone.

Performance activity and pay perception:

> 49% of musicians perform 1-3 shows monthly locally.

> 44% tour 1-3 shows monthly; 30% tour zero shows.

> 87% consider their pay "stagnated."

Industry structure:

> 18.2% of respondents worked in music industry less than two years.

> 90% of music businesses employ 1-5 people.

Venue challenges:

> 53% of venue owners rate monthly rent as having "extreme/strong impact."

> 62% report APD sound ordinance enforcement significantly affects business.

Economic impact framing:

> Music contributes $1.6 billion annually to Austin's economy, ranking in the top 10 economic contributors.

## Why it matters

The most numerically detailed contemporaneous account of the census. Supplies the census budget ($45,000), total respondent count (3,968), musician subsample (1,883), the 1,288-under-$10,000 count, the 75%-below-average-wage comparison against Austin's $48,150 average annual wage, and the 87% "stagnated pay" figure. Directly juxtaposes the $1.6B impact figure with musician-level poverty.

## Note on a related figure

Search-result summaries also attribute to Chronicle coverage an Austin musician average of $23,371 annually, of which $15,504 comes from creative work, with 81% holding side jobs, and a per-gig norm of roughly $50-$100 per musician unchanged for 40-plus years. Those specific figures were NOT confirmed verbatim in the fetched text of this article and should be sourced to the census report or to the 2022 KUT per-gig story before use.
