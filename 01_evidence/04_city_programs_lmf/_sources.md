# Sources — City of Austin music and creative-economy programs

Beat: City of Austin programs and money for working musicians (Live Music Fund and related).
All items retrieved **2026-08-01** unless noted. Files live in this folder; extracted plain text for every PDF is in `_txt/`; bulk open-data downloads are in `socrata/`.

Evidence tiers used below:
- **P** = primary city document (ordinance, resolution, guidelines, official award list, audit, city open-data extract)
- **S** = secondary reporting (used only where no primary document was located)

## Open items — require a public-records request or manual retrieval

1. **Applicant counts and demographic/genre breakdowns by cohort.** The FY23 and FY24 Power BI dashboards are the only city artifacts that appear to carry these, and they are JavaScript-gated with no static export. No equivalent dashboard was published for FY26. **PIR: request the underlying applicant and awardee tabulations (applications received, scored, funded; award tier; council district; and any race/ethnicity, gender, and genre fields collected) for every LMF cycle FY2023–FY2026.**
2. **FY25 disposition.** The city reports zero FY25 awards. **PIR: request documentation of what happened to the FY25 appropriation** — whether it lapsed, carried forward, or was rolled into the FY26 cycle, which would explain the FY26 jump to $7.14M.
3. **Audited Live Music Fund balance by fiscal year.** My cumulative revenue-minus-expenditure figure is a proxy built from FY2021 forward and does not reconcile to the ~$3.2M balance reported in press coverage for September 2025. **PIR: request the ACFR fund-balance schedule for the Live Music Fund and the Iconic Venue Fund, FY2020–FY2026.**
4. **Music & Entertainment Division budget and headcount time series.** Only FY2026 is available in open data. **PIR or manual: pull the Approved Budget volumes FY2019–FY2026** for the EDD/ACME department pages showing Music & Entertainment FTEs and appropriations. Note that the program moved from EDD to the new Office of Arts, Culture, Music and Entertainment (ACME) on 2025-02-24, which breaks the series.
5. **Third-party administration contract with the Long Center.** Contract value, term, and fee structure not located. The Open Budget "Consultant-others" object is a proxy. **PIR: request the executed agreement and amendments.**
6. **Austin Music Disaster Relief Fund final accounting.** Ordinance 20200423-067 (2020-04-23) appropriated $1.5M for $1,000 grants distributed via MusiCares. **Number of recipients and dollars actually disbursed were not found in any city document.** PIR needed.
7. **SAVES / Live Music Venue Preservation Fund primary documents.** Ordinance 20201001-052 and Resolution 20200103-013 PDFs not captured; award lists not located.
8. **Red River extended-hours pilot evaluation memo.** The outcome statistics circulate only through news coverage of an EDD memo (Kevin Johns). The memo itself was not found in EDIMS.
9. **Agent of Change ordinance final status.** Traced from 2015 through a September 2024 council agenda item; whether a final ordinance was adopted is unresolved.
10. **CSAP award lists for FY20, FY21, FY22, FY24, FY25.** Only FY19, FY23 and FY26 lists are published.
11. **Busking / street-performance rules.** No dedicated city ordinance page located. The `austintexas.gov/page/musician-loading-permits` URL now 404s after the site redesign, so the musician loading-zone permit terms ($25 per permit, 30-minute zones, venue-purchased) rest on **S** reporting and cached descriptions rather than a live city page.
