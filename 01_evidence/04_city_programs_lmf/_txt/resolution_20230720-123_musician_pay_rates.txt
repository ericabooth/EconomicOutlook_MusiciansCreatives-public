                        RESOLUTION NO. 20230720-123

       WHEREAS, the City o f Austin is known as the Live Music Capital o f the
World, and musicians are crucial and vibrant contributors to the character o f the
City; and

       WHEREAS, it has been shown by sources, such as the ATX Music Industry
Census and Needs Assessment, that musicians in Austin struggle to earn a living

wage which keeps pace with the rising cost of living in the City. As a result, Austin
is vulnerable to losing musicians and crucial parts of its music industry due to
unaffordability; and

      WHEREAS, the Imagine Austin Comprehensive Plan states that the
creative sector, including the music industry, is a cornerstone of the City's identity
and economic prosperity, but that the creative community struggles with quality of
life in the City; and

      WHEREAS, the City employs musicians through a variety of programs
administered by the Economic Development, Aviation, Parks and Recreation, and
Library Departments, amongst others; and

      WHEREAS, setting an appropriate living wage for musicians differs from
other pro fessions because o f the significant amount o f un-compensated preparation
work musicians must do and the relatively short performance times for which
musicians are compensated; and

       WHEREAS, the City's prevailing pay rate for musicians was set at $100 per
hour in 2012 and then $150 per hour in 2016, and this pay rate has been matched
by partner entities such as Cap Metro; and

      WHEREAS, the Austin Music Commission passed Recommendation
20220307-3c in March 2022, recommending that the hourly rate for City musical
performances increase to $200 per hour per musician; and

        WHEREAS, the Economic Development Department has since adopted and
has been paying this standard rate of $200 per hour per musician for City musical
performances; and

      WHEREAS, through mechanical and live entertainment musical licensing
programs such as KUT at Airport, Electric Lady Bird, and Music on Hold, the City
also works to fairly compensate musicians; and

        WHEREAS, a standard rate o f pay for City musical performances would
assist in raising the universal standard for musical performance pay across the
City; NOW, THEREFORE,
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF AUSTIN:

        The City acknowledges the needs of its musicians to earn a living wage in
order to maintain the cultural and artistic fabric of this City. The City intends to
remain the Live Music Capital of the World by supporting the local music industry
and its musicians through regular investments, including by means of paid

performances.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED:

        The City places a premium on the talent of its musicians and hereby
establishes its pay rate for musicians for a live City music performance of no less
than:

              $200 per musician for groups of up to six people

                                      Page 2 0 f 3
                $150 per musician for groups between seven and ten people

                $1500 total for groups of musicians of ten or more

         This musician pay rate is to be used by all City departments directly hiring
musicians and for awards for live music performance from the Live Music Fund.
The Austin Music Commission should regularly review the musician pay rate for
future adjustments and make recommendations that consider the totality of
compensation and performance opportunities.
         In addition to the musician pay rate, the City Manager and Austin Music
Commission should also review fair compensation for licensing by the City.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED:

         The City Manager is directed to develop a voluntary program that markets
and celebrates community partners that adhere to the City s pay rate for musicians,
