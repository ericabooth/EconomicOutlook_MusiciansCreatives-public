 1   AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 9-2 RELATING TO THE HOURS AN
 2   OUTDOOR MUSIC VENUE LOCATED IN THE RED RIVER CULTURAL
 3   DISTRICT MAY OPERATE SOUND EQUIPMENT.

 4         BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF AUSTIN:
 5   PART 1. FINDINGS.

 6   The council finds the following:

 7                 (1) The Music Commission’s Recommendation No. 20161107-4 highlighted the
 8                     impact of the Red River Cultural District on local live music industry.

 9                 (2) The City is committed to ensuring that live music venues along Red River
10                     Street continue to serve as incubators for local musicians; and to protecting
11                     the quality of life for residents and visitors.

12                 (3) Ordinance No. 20170126-019 created a six month pilot program that
13                     authorized extended decibel limits and hours to operate sound equipment at
14                     outdoor music venues located along Red River Street between 6th and 12th
15                     Streets.

16                 (4) Ordinance No. 20171019-007 extended the pilot program until April 30,
17                     2018.

19   River Cultural District” to read as follows and to renumber the remaining
20   definitions accordingly:

 7   PART 3. Subsection (A) of Section 9-2-30 (Decibel Limits for Outdoor Music) is
 8   amended to read as follows:
 9   § 9-2-30 Decibel Limits for Outdoor Music
10        (A) Unless a more restrictive decibel limit is required by a temporary event
11            impact plan, a condition on an outdoor music venue permit, or by another
12            provision of this code, sound equipment for which a permit has been
13            issued under Subpart B (Live Music Permits) may be operated at the
14            following decibel levels:
15                 (1) Up to 85 decibels between 10:00 a.m. and:
16                            (a) 10:30 p.m. on Sunday through Wednesday;
17                            (b) 11:00 p.m. on Thursday; or
18                            (c) 12:00 midnight on Friday or Saturday.
19                 (2) Up to 85 decibels at an outdoor music venue with an occupancy
20                     capacity of 600 or fewer people located in the Warehouse District or
21                     Sixth Street District, between 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 a.m.
22                 (3) Up to 85 decibels during spring festival season, between 10:00 a.m.
23                     and 2:00 a.m.
24                 (4) Up to 85 decibels at an outdoor music venue located in the Red River
25                     Cultural District, between 10:00 a.m. and:
26                            (a) 10:30 p.m. on Sunday through Wednesday;
27                            (b) 12:00 midnight on Thursday; or
28                            (c) 1:00 a.m. on Friday or Saturday.
